# Aksoy City — notes for Claude

SimCity BuildIt-style grid city builder, played on iPhone 16 Pro Safari. Live: https://mustafaaksoy566.github.io/aksoy-city/ (repo mustafaaksoy566/aksoy-city, pushing to main deploys via .github/workflows).

## Working with the user
- Speak **Turkish**, simply, step by step. The user does not code. After every batch, list each request and what was done (they often say "hepsini yap, hiçbirini unutma").
- They usually ask "devam et daha fazlasını ekle": implement every listed item, then add something extra, test it, commit + push, and report in Turkish.
- Everything in the game is drawn/synthesized in code: NO external models, textures, sounds or fonts (the user asked for this to avoid licences, 2026-10-13). Draw new things procedurally; do not add downloaded assets. Ask before anything paid, any account, or any software install. Never create accounts, enter tokens/passwords, change system or security settings, or accept terms for them.
- Deploys: GitHub API (actions/runs) shows the Pages run status; or fetch the live page and grep for the new `index-*.js` bundle name.
- Materials are never shown as emoji only (some phones and Windows lack glyphs such as 🪵): cards use productPic pictures (InfoCard.cost has pic) or names (costText, catalog subtitles, storage float text); level-up unlocks use `mat:<id>` thumbs.
- Music (audio.ts startMusic): generative calm music (pads, bass, pentatonic marimba, convolver reverb), its own bus straight to the destination, so the effects switch does not mute it; Settings has a Müzik switch (pref aksoy-city-music); setMusicNight thins the melody at night. Settings also opens the "Nasıl oynanır?" guide (ui.ts HELP/openHelp), shown once automatically on a brand-new game (pref aksoy-city-help). The storage card sells one item per tap at half its price (city.sell/sellPrice). Quests also include land (buy a plot) and landmark (build from the Tarihi menu).
- Achievements (achievements.ts ACHIEVEMENTS: chains of tiers, value(city)): city.achieved {id: claimed tiers} (save `a`); trophy button #ach in #side (badge = ready count), card 'ach' = info.describeAchievements (quest rows with claimed flag; "Hepsini al" runs act.achieve('*')); main.checkAchievements every 2 s toasts newly ready ones (not the ones already ready at load). Sounds 'chime' and 'shutter' (audio.ts).
- Daily gift (gift.ts): city.gift {d: local date, s: day 1..7} (save `g`); main.checkGift after the product pictures are ready and on visibilitychange; a missed day restarts at 1, day 7 wraps; rewards scale with city level, materials that do not fit in storage are paid in money; a brand-new city skips its first day (help opens instead).
- History graph: city.history [[pop, money]] pushed when the HUD day turns (save `h`, last 90) → InfoCard.chart (city card: population, budget card: money; ui chartHtml SVG).
- Photo mode (#photo in #cam): body.photo-on hides the HUD, ui.setPhotoBar (shutter, close, time chips); photoTod only changes the look (dayNight, fireworks, music), traffic keeps the real time; shootPhoto renders, crops the visible part of the canvas (it overhangs the safe areas), stamps city name/population, shows a preview with navigator.share (files) or a download link. Taps and long-press do nothing in photo mode.
- Seasons (season.ts): a year is 28 HUD days, 7 per season (seasonOf(dayNumber)); city card shows the season, a toast announces it. installSnow() (top of main.ts, before the first render) sets MeshStandardMaterial.prototype.onBeforeCompile so every standard material whitens up-facing faces by the shared uniform SEASON_U.snow (world normal from viewMatrix after normal_fragment_maps); noSnow(mat) exempts roads (road kit, view roadDeco/CUL asphalt and paint, highway asphalt/lines) and sea/river water, and batcher keeps those materials separate ('keep'). Materials with their own onBeforeCompile must use withSnow(mat, own) and their own customProgramCacheKey. Winter: Weather.winter makes precipitation snow (Points flakes, more often); main.updateSeason accumulates snowCover while it snows and melts it after winter or when weather is off. Autumn: SEASON_U.autumn shifts green leaf colours to yellow/orange/red (autumnPatch): city tree crowns use leafMat (userData.leaf → batcher 'leaf' group with leafMergedMat), pine/cypress use needleMat and stay green; forests use forestDecid/forestLeaf. Menu thumbnails render with snow and autumn at 0 (thumbs.ts). Settings switch is "Yağmur ve kar".
- Citizen requests (info.ts citizenWants, hall card = happiness button): houses grouped by upgradeBlocker (park, shop, services with the missing ones, decor, boulevard, metro, utilities, road) plus fires, damaged homes and jammed roads, sorted by weight; each tile's "Göster" runs act.showSpot(cells): camera tweens to the cell nearest the screen centre and opens its card.
- Water levels (2026-10-14): land is visibly above water. water.ts SEA_Y = -0.28, RIVER_Y = -0.24 (city ground CITY_Y = -0.03). terrain.ts shore(c) drops steeply right at the waterline c = -0.5 (the boundary between the city's sand row 18 and sea row 17; COAST_Z itself is the middle of row 17), sea colours use coast + 0.5; extra grid lines at the coast and the river banks keep the banks steep. The city river bed (inRiverBed: corners whose four tiles are river or sea, at least one river) is RIVER_Y - 0.12, also just outside the city where the river leaves it. view.ts river(): stone quay walls (quayMat canvas ashlar, quayBox UVs) with a light coping on both banks instead of sand strips; the mouth plume material is a clone, so it needs its own noSnow. Models at the water use SEA_Y (marina, lighthouse, Kız Kulesi incl. its deck lamps, port ship and quay wall, sky ships).
- Bridges (view.ts bridge(mask, waterY, hung)): piers (cap beam along the flow, two round columns, footings) reach the water; tiles of a suspension span (inSpan: ≥3 straight sea road tiles) get a hung steel box girder instead. suspension(): Bosphorus-like white tapered towers on concrete footings with three portal beams, thick main cables from end anchorage blocks over tower saddles, zigzag inclined hangers, red beacons; LED spheres on cables and tower legs (MeshBasicMaterial 'led', transparent so it stays separate) change colour at night via updateBridgeLights (called in view.update; night from BULB.emissiveIntensity).
- River tour boats (riverboats.ts RiverBoats, updated in main frame): three small boats run the river path (mouth → corner → west city edge) and back, keeping to the right lane (side lerps at the turnarounds), under the bridges (piers are outside the lanes, boat tops stay below the cap beams).
- Model registry: assets.ts makeModel(path) builds `proc/...` models (trees.ts, towers.ts houses/towers, roads.ts road kit, props.ts hydrant/construction props). Boats: boats.ts makeBoat. Sounds: audio.ts synthesizes them with WebAudio. Font: system rounded font (ui-rounded = SF Rounded on iPhone).

## Architecture (src/)
- `config.ts`: all constants and catalog (BUILD_GROUPS, TOOL_INFO, COST, UNLOCK, FOOTPRINT, DECOR, INDUSTRY, SHOPS, MAKER_LEVELS, RES_LEVELS 8 levels, ROAD_GRADES, TRAFFIC).
- Map: `SIZE=64` cols × `ROWS=80` rows (`NORTH=16` sea rows on the north; grown east and south from 48×64 on 2026-10-13, new land beyond the river is bought as plots). `idx = z*SIZE + x`; world pos = `(x - X0 + 0.5, z - Z0 + 0.5)`, `X0=24`, `Z0=40` (fixed, so old tiles keep their world place; never use `SIZE/2` for world positions). City edges in world: x -24..40, z -40..40 (terrain.ts WEST/EAST/ZN/ZS; the highway south of the city and the clover follow ZS). `RIVER` [41,42] and `RIVER_ROWS` [57,58] are fixed numbers. Plots 8×8. Sea for `z < COAST_ROW` except the `ISLAND` ellipse; roads over sea = suspension bridges (`SEA_BRIDGE`). River columns `RIVER`, rows `RIVER_ROWS`.
- `city.ts`: game state, rules, traffic BFS (`updateTraffic`, `congestion`), road grades (`Tile.grade` 1 = Bulvar, 2 = Cadde with 4 lanes), `streetOf`/`upgradeStreet`, maker upgrades, save/load. Save is v5 with `rows`, `rg:2`, `o`. Load migrates old saves (maps narrower than OLD_W=48 were centred in 48; 48 and wider are west-aligned, so shiftX 0; z shift, com → res, refunds for trade and buildings on sea). Roads and rails on water are kept.
- `view.ts`: builds meshes per tile (static chunks merged by `batcher.ts`), road decorations (`roadExtras`, `junctionExtras`, `culDeSac`, `signalPost`), bridges (`suspension`), construction sites, ghost road preview (`showRoadPreview`), traffic overlay.
- `traffic.ts`: cars and buses (lanes, queueing, traffic lights, cul-de-sac U-turns, parking lots and garages, see below) and walkers (leave and enter house doors). `signals.ts`: traffic-light timing.
- `input.ts`: tile input; road and rail drawing follows the finger path (`follow`). `camera.ts`: custom rig (pan, pinch, twist, two-finger tilt, PC keys).
- `terrain.ts` (world outside and under the city, coast, island, river, highway tunnel), `water.ts`, `trees.ts` (procedural trees), `towers.ts` (procedural towers, villas, apartments: `proc/` paths), `historic.ts` (Eiffel, Pisa, Galata, Colosseum, Pyramid, stadium), `landmarks.ts` (mosque, lighthouse, marina, clock tower, `makeDecor`), `zoo.ts` (naturalistic zoo: blob enclosures, painted animal atlas, wandering animals via userData.animals step), `highway.ts`, `rails.ts`/`trains.ts`, `sky.ts`/`planes.ts`, `daynight.ts`, `weather.ts`, `ui.ts`/`info.ts` (HUD, build catalog, info cards), `thumbs.ts` (menu pictures).
- Housing: RES_LEVELS levels 1-4 are cottage → family house → small apartment → midrise (`houses.ts` + towers.ts midrisePaths); levels 5-8 are the professional skyscrapers of `skyline.ts` (skylinePaths(height, designs, seed) → `proc/sky-<design>-<height>`; 5: green/wave/terrace, 6: twist/crystal/ribbon, 7: diagrid/twins/sail/holding, 8: burj/deco/pyramid; SKY_NAMES for the Konut menu). Upgrading keeps `t.variant` so the model order stays.
- skyline.ts: finer facade textures than towers.ts (FL 0.055 floor, BW 0.05 window, 16x16 cells on 384 px canvases; styles curtain/silver/gold/dark/ribbon/stone/resi/green; night lights lit in runs along floors), materials cached and named window; geometry helpers sides() (prism with per-floor UVs, taper about (0,0), slanted topY), capGeo (with holes), offset(); lobby base(), roof() parapet ring, helipad(), lightRing() (lamp crowns glow at night), RED_LIGHT aviation beacons always on, glowSign() lit sign. Shiny metal (STEEL/GOLD) carries a white map so mergeStatic keeps its metalness. DOORS/HELIPADS are getters that build the model on first use.
- World wonders (`wonders.ts`, registered into landmarks.ts EXTRA_DECOR so makeDecor finds them; WONDER_KINDS feeds the menu pictures): Özgürlük Anıtı (liberty, redrawn: Fort Wood star, granite pedestal with loggias, copper figure with a parametric folded robe coloured by vertex colours, gilded torch that glows at night), Kız Kulesi (maiden, ON_SEA: all cells must be sea, no road needed, tourism counts without a road, load keeps it on the sea; islet with rocks, quay, tower with lantern and Turkish flag, bobbing boat via userData.bob), Ayasofya (hagia), Tac Mahal (taj), Big Ben (bigben: canvas Gothic facade and clock dials that glow at night), Parthenon, Chichen Itza (chichen), Sidney Opera Binası (opera, NEEDS_WATER so it faces the water; tiled sail shells with recessed bronze glass), Stonehenge, Kapadokya (cappadocia 3x3: tuff fairy chimneys with STRATA layer texture and basalt caps, carved windows in CAVE_WIN 'window' material that glow at night, ridge cave hotel, vineyard, crew cars, lying envelopes; six hot-air balloons animated by userData.animals step: they rise one by one in the day and drift around the site at 3.5-4.6 world height, land on their pads at night (night read from BULB.emissiveIntensity), burner flame and envelope glow; balloon meshes cast no shadows because the shadow map is not redrawn every frame), Pamukkale (pamukkale 3x3: five travertine terrace bands narrowing up the hillside, scalloped fronts with turquoise pools and hanging drips, Hierapolis theatre, colonnade and Antique Pool on the plateau, side stairs), Truva Atı (troy 2x2: plank-textured wooden horse with windows and ladder on a platform, sloped Troy walls and ruined tower), Efes (ephesus 2x2: Celsus Library facade with paired columns, four statues in niches, three doors, triangular/segmental pediments, ruined reading hall, Curetes Street columns). - Service map (main.ts renderServiceMap, view.showServiceMap, ui setServiceBar): tapping a utility/service building (or the ⚡/💧 HUD) whitens the city, coverage tiles green, covered houses green / uncovered red; arrows cycle categories.
- Move/rotate buildings: long-press (450 ms) or card "✋ Taşı" lifts a building; after dragging it waits with a ✓ / ✗ / ↻ bar (ui setPlaceBar). ✗ restores place and facing, ✓ commits via city.moveBuilding(from, x, z, facing). ↻ turns only to road-facing sides (no card button any more). Code: input.ts hold/grab → main startMove/confirmMove/cancelMove/rotateMove, view.startMove/moveLifted/turnLifted/endMove.
- Parking lots upgrade to 2-5 storey garages (PARKING_LEVELS); upgraded lots act as garages in traffic.
- Metro: city.metro (Uint8Array tunnels, saved as `m`), tool `tunnel` drawn like roads, Metro button toggles the underground view (metro.ts MetroView: veil, street ghosts, coloured lines, station rings, trains). A line is active when it links ≥2 metro stations; active stations get bigger radius/happiness/transit (METRO_LINE), stations upgrade via METRO_LEVELS.
- Highway: roundabout at the city entrance; south-west of the city a cloverleaf (HW.clover): N-S motorway on the ground, E-W motorway (HW.west → HW.east) on a bridge (bHeight), loops + outer ramps from cloverQuadrant(k); route parts carry per-point heights (Lane.hs).
- City entrance: tiles x=-1,-2 outside the map are drawn with view.entranceTile (same as city roads) in main.syncHighway; highway gate routes exist per city lane (HighwayTraffic.setCityLanes ← traffic.entranceLanes), handoff is position- and lane-continuous; leaving cars wait at the edge if highway.canLeave() is false; cars on tile 0 heading west always exit (no U-turn); buses come from/return to the highway (sendBus, type -1 drawn by HighwayTraffic.busMesh).
- Bus and train are procedural (`vehicles.ts` busModel/trainCar; sky.makeBus delegates). Stadium "Aksoy Arena" in historic.ts stadium(); ski resort rebuilt in ski.ts (lodge moved +0.5/-1.0 from terrain LODGE).
- Camera clearance: view.topNear(x, z) (tall objects > 1.2 recorded in view.build) → rig.clearance; CameraRig.keepAbove lifts the camera above towers so it never clips inside one.
- Traffic deadlock rules: a car waiting at red does not claim the junction (occupy only on green); lastJ occupancy only on the tile right after the junction; blocked() returns 'ped' for walkers and box.ped lets walkers pass yielding cars (3 s cap); highway toCity cars wait in the tunnel unless traffic.wantsCars().
- Production (production.ts ProductionPanel, wired in main openProduction/closeProduction): tapping a factory/shop first collects ready items (if any), otherwise opens the workshop: camera tweens to a low front close-up (focusOn: least-blocked yaw, view.setHidden hides buildings blocking the view, minDistance 3; closing restores the saved pose), HUD/icons hidden (body.prod-on, view.showIcons). Bottom bench = a conveyor machine (steel frame, rubber belt with rollers, motors with spinning gears and status LEDs, hazard stripe) carrying job pods: product on a white plate inside a conic progress ring (run green, wait grey with queue number, ready gold with ✓ and Topla, empty dashed, "+" buy slot), plus a shelf of product cards (tap or drag onto the building) whose footer says Üret / Malzeme eksik / Bant dolu. Finished jobs wait in the building (city.readyAt/collectAt; view ready bubbles, tap → view.readyBubbleAt); idle makers show "Zz". city: Job.start, Tile.slots (EXTRA_SLOTS), speedUp/cancelJob/buySlot. Item pictures: productPic() (3D icons from items.ts via thumbRenderer env, emoji fallback). New shops garden/cafe/electro in shops.ts; new raw chem/sugar/glass/parts.
- Walkers (traffic.ts Walkers) walk on either sidewalk (Person.side 1 = right of travel, -1 = left; walkPos). At a junction a turn toward their own corner keeps the side; a turn the other way (or a U-turn) crosses the incoming arm at the junction edge and flips the side (sideAfter: right turn -> 1, left turn -> -1), so there are no diagonal crossings. Every walker has a goal door (doorOf: a straight road tile next to the building): morning home -> work/stop, day home <-> shops/parks/fun, evening work/shops -> home, night -> home (spawn by dayPhase); BFS fields from the shared Router guide pickDir; at the goal tile they enter (or cross the street; on a barrier cadde they go on to the junction and come back). Walkers check the right light before crossing (lightLimit). A walker yields to a car that waits for them for at most 3 s and then keeps going (the counter used to reset -> bus and walkers waited for each other forever); a car that waits > 6 s for walkers passes them for 1.5 s (pedPass).
- Junction signals: one pole per approach (view signalPost(tile, mask)) with red/amber/green (signals.ts SIGNAL_MATS.amber); street lamps are procedural (view streetLight): every straight plain road tile has one, alternating sides (each sidewalk a lamp every 2 tiles; on a zebra tile it stands 0.3 off the crossing), plus one on the outer curb of plain corners and on the flat side of plain T junctions; bulvar median lamps every 2nd tile, cadde median lamps every 2nd tile.
- Days: the economy step (tax, upkeep, growth, `city.day`) is still DAY_SECONDS = 4 s, but the HUD day is one day-night cycle (config dayNumber: DAY_STEPS = 30 steps = 2 min, the counter turns at dawn). Cards that say günlük / /gün show perDay(value) = value × 30; the fire countdown is shown in seconds.
- Traffic (traffic.ts): cars never spawn mid-road. Junction right-of-way: a car stops at STOP if a conflicting movement (conflicts()) is inside/committed in the junction, if its exit has no room (don't block the box) or a conflicting car has waited longer on green; one manoeuvre per parking lot at a time; long-stuck cars fade out (vanish) instead of driving through others. Cadde lanes WIDE_LANES [0.115, 0.3]. Look-ahead boxes stop cars/walkers from overlapping (blocked() rejects far boxes by distance first: it was 2.3 ms/frame).
- Car jobs (traffic.ts, SimCity-like): dayPhase(tod) morning 0.93-0.15 / day -0.45 / evening -0.68 / night (one day = 2 min; a trip leg takes ~30 s, so going home starts before dark). Cars only drive to buildings with their own lot (sites.ts). Morning: the owner walks out of a house door to the car in a public lot (walkPath) and drives to work (visit(place, -1): parks in the building's lot, the driver walks to the door and waits in Traffic.inside until evening), tower residents drive out of their garage; van/pickup owners first load at a factory/depot and unload at a shop. Day: the buildings' own trucks (fleet) do factory/depot -> shop/factory -> back to their own bay (cargoRun), errands to shops/fun places, highway visitors visit a place then exit. Evening: workers walk out of the door to their car and park at the lot nearest their home ('home' Dest -> homeDest), the driver walks home through the house door. Night: few taxis. Places lists come from scan(); choosePlace weights by exp(-distance/9) so trips stay short. Car size CAR_SCALE 0.6 / BUS_SCALE 0.68 (cars.ts) so they fit the 0.16 cadde lanes; carModel uses it too.
- Own lots and doors (sites.ts SITES, single source for models and traffic): shops (2x2 since 2026-10-13, stores.ts STORES2, lot in front, zebra to the door), factory (yellow truck bays + car bays + office door), hospital, school, and police/fire/depot (2x2 in stations.ts; fire trucks use the three open garage doors, FireStation.doors {at, road} via Traffic.frontRoad). OLD_SITES keeps the 1x1 police/fire/depot of old saves (siteOf picks by fp). Coordinates are site-local world units (n x n footprint centred, +z = front/road); models wrap content in a group scaled 1/(n*0.96) so cars/people match the real size. Traffic.siteLot builds Slot {pos, road (tile in front of the bay), walk (bay -> walkway -> door -> 0.09 inside)}; arriveAt parks, the driver walks in (Walk.hide 'in' = full size, no shrinking) and releaseInside walks out ('out'). Fleet bays hold the building's own police/ambulance/truck/garbage; fleet vehicles only return to their own lot (else exit) and refillFleet (in scan) puts a new one in a bay whose vehicle vanished. A parked visitor keeps p.trip until it drives off (counts as away). Houses/towers register door points in towers.ts DOORS (houses.ts register(prefix, make, door)); entryPath uses them. Old 1x1 buildings whose FOOTPRINT grew (shops, police, fire, depot; fp undefined) keep the old model: city.growShops on load grows them in place into a free 2x2, otherwise the card shows "🅿️ Taşı · otoparklı 2x2 olsun" and moveBuilding grows them (old cells are taken BEFORE fp changes, or the neighbours get deleted).
- Roads: roads.ts road kit (straight, crossing, corner, T, junction; asphalt 0.035, lines 0.0365, curbs 0.05; SimCity-like navy asphalt ROAD_COLORS). Dead ends are drawn by culDeSac() in view.ts (half road + rounded end).

Shops can sit together in one district; the rule only adds options, it never takes a shop away from a house.

- Flicker (z-fighting) rule: two faces of different colour must never share a plane. Keep stacked tops/sides >= 0.002 local apart, make slats/stripes narrower than the door/box they sit on, and use parts.ts `frame`/`basin` (open rim + inset water/soil) instead of a thin plate on a solid box. Thin edge walls are outward planes without tops.
- Buildings can only be rotated so their front faces a road (city.facingsAt / openSides; station -> rail, port/beach/marina -> water; no road around -> any side). The card has only "Taşı"; rotating is the ↻ in the move bar, and a dragged building turns to face the road by itself (main faceRoad).
- Terrain (terrain.ts heightAt): near every road the ground is forced to road level after the river valley (the rising west river used to lift the ground over the motorway); the clover's west motorway runs to the horizon (HW.west -118). - Underpass tool (Ulaşım → Alt geçit, BuildTool 'underpass'): drawn like a road but straight (input cellsBetween); city.underpassPlan(cells) → the two end cells become ramps (straight cadde, outer neighbour a road, not a junction), the cells between are covered (junction not required); empty cells get a new cadde and plain roads are widened to cadde via apply('avenue'); a single tap covers that tile with its two neighbours as ramps. Cost UNDERPASS.cost + extend per extra covered tile plus the widening. apply('underpass') → drawUnderpass; main preview shows the whole plan and its error in the chip.
- Underpass runs: Tile.under (axis) can cover several tiles (junctions + straight cadde); city.underRun/extendPlan/extendUnderpass (card "Alt geçidi uzat" on a ramp), switchUnderpass (other avenue at a cadde x cadde junction).
